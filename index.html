Privacy Policy

InvoiceCraft

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Compose R.A Studio developed InvoiceCraft as an invoicing tool for small businesses and freelancers. It lets the user create invoices, estimates, quotes, and receipts as PDF documents with their logo and signature, manage clients and saved products and services, record payments, convert estimates into invoices, receive payment reminders, view reports, export data to CSV, and make backups.

All the information entered in the application is stored only on the user's device. The application does not have its own server (backend), does not require creating an account, does not ask for user registration, and does not use any authentication method, such as logging in with Google or any other provider. Its invoicing features work without an internet connection.

To remain free of charge, the application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices about advertising where the law requires it. Users who prefer no advertisements can buy InvoiceCraft Premium, an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play that removes them. These services never receive the business information, the documents, or the client data recorded in the application. The application does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own.

Information privacy and security are a priority for Compose R.A Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, where it is kept, and what choices and rights the user has.

Information Stored on the Device

The information created by the user, including, but not limited to:

  • Business profile: business name, legal name, tax ID, email address, phone number, address, city, website, logo, and payment instructions
  • Signature: the signature the user draws in the application to show on their documents
  • Clients: name or company, contact person, email address, phone number, tax ID, address, city, and private notes
  • Saved products and services: name, description, code or SKU, unit, price, and tax
  • Documents: invoices, estimates, quotes, and receipts with their items, prices, discounts, taxes, withholdings, notes, terms, dates, and status
  • Payments: amount, date, payment method, and reference
  • Settings: country, currency, number and date formats, taxes and withholdings, document numbering, PDF template and color, default texts, language, theme, reminder settings, and app lock settings

is stored exclusively in the application's private storage on the user's device. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, or have access to this information.

The data remains under the exclusive control of the user, unless they decide to share a document, a report, or a message, or to make a backup using the features available in the application.

To suggest the country, currency, and tax settings when the application is set up, it reads the time zone and the region configured on the phone. This does not use the device's location (GPS) or the SIM card, happens on the device, and is not sent anywhere.

Information About the User's Clients

When the user records their clients, they enter personal information about other people. This information is stored only on the user's device and Compose R.A Studio never receives it.

The user decides what information to record about their clients and is responsible for handling it in accordance with the laws that apply to them, for example by recording only the information needed to bill their clients. The application helps by keeping this information on the device, by allowing it to be edited or deleted at any time, and by offering an optional app lock.

What the Application Does Not Do

InvoiceCraft:

  • Does not require creating an account
  • Does not ask for the user's name, email, or phone number to use the application
  • Does not use authentication through Google, Facebook, or other services
  • Does not sync the recorded information with its own servers or any other server
  • Does not process payments from the user's clients: recording a payment only stores a note of a payment received outside the application
  • Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own
  • Does not access the device's location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS messages
  • Does not access the photo gallery: when the user adds a logo, Android's photo picker gives the application access only to the image the user selects
  • Does not send, sell, or share the business information, the documents, or the client data recorded by the user with anyone, including advertisers

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services. They may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The application shows full-screen advertisements (interstitials) served by Google AdMob. They never appear when the application opens, during the welcome and setup screens, or during the first 24 hours after the application is installed. They appear only occasionally, after the user has completed several actions, such as saving a document, sharing or saving a PDF, or recording a payment, with a minimum time between two advertisements. They are shown at a natural pause, when the user returns to a main screen or opens a document after saving it, and never on top of the share menu or in the middle of a task.

To serve and measure advertisements, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and, where the user allows it, personalize them, AdMob may collect:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information
  • Interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Performance and diagnostic information of the advertising SDK, including crash logs

On devices that support them, AdMob may also use Android's advertising APIs of the Privacy Sandbox (such as Topics and Attribution), which the user can manage or turn off in the device settings.

The business information, the documents, and the client data recorded in the application are never shared with AdMob and are not used to choose advertisements. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (Privacy Choices)

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform provided by Google, to request and record privacy choices about advertising as required in each region. These choices are stored on the device and passed to Google's advertising services.

Google Play Billing (InvoiceCraft Premium)

InvoiceCraft Premium is an optional one-time purchase whose only effect is removing all advertisements; every other feature is available without paying. The purchase is processed entirely by Google Play: payment details are entered in Google Play and are never received by the application or by Compose R.A Studio. Google handles the purchase, its receipt, and any refund according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

To know whether Premium is active, the application asks Google Play which purchases the Google account on the device owns and stores only an indicator (active or not) on the device. The purchase can be restored on another device signed in with the same Google account. While Premium is active, the application does not request or show advertisements.

The user can review, change, or withdraw their consent at any time in More → Ad privacy.

Where Google's privacy message is shown, the user can opt out from it or later from More → Ad privacy. The user can also use the device controls described below. After opting out, Google restricts how it processes that information, and advertisements are not personalized based on it.

Sharing Initiated by the User

Some features open other applications. They are used only when the user requests them, and the user can review the content before sending it:

  • Documents (PDF): invoices, estimates, quotes, and receipts can be shared through the Android share menu, with the client's email address and a prepared message filled in when available, or saved in the location the user chooses.
  • Printing: documents are printed through Android's print service and the printer the user selects.
  • Payment reminders and contact buttons: the WhatsApp, email, and call buttons open the corresponding application with the client's phone number or email address and, where applicable, a prepared message. Nothing is sent until the user confirms it in that application.
  • Reports (CSV): the exported file is saved in the location the user chooses.

Once information is shared, it is handled by the application or service the user chose, according to its own privacy policy. To share or print a document, the application creates a temporary copy of the PDF in its private cache, which Android may clear at any time and which is deleted when the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Backups

From More → Backup and restore, the user can create a backup file (.zip) with all the information of the application: business profile, clients, saved items, documents, payments, settings, logo, and signature. The file is created only when the user requests it and is saved in the location the user chooses, such as the device's storage or a cloud storage service. If that location is synchronized with a cloud storage service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

The backup file is not encrypted and contains the personal information of the user's clients, so it should be kept in a safe place. Restoring a backup replaces all the information currently stored in the application.

If backup is enabled in the device's Android settings, Android may also include the application's database, settings, logo, and signature in the backup of the user's Google account, or copy them when moving to a new device. That backup is managed by Google under the user's account, and Compose R.A Studio has no access to it. It can be turned off from the device's backup settings.

Payment Reminders and Notifications

When payment reminders are turned on, the application checks on the device, once a day, which invoices are due soon or overdue and which estimates and quotes are about to expire, and shows a notification. These notifications may include the document number, the client's name, the amount, and the due date, and may appear on the lock screen depending on the device's notification settings.

Reminders can be turned off at any time in More → Payment reminders, and notifications can also be disabled from the device settings.

App Lock and Biometric Authentication

InvoiceCraft offers an optional app lock, in More → Security, that asks for the device's fingerprint, face unlock, or screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password) when the application is opened, depending on the device's capabilities.

Authentication is processed entirely by the Android operating system, using the device's secure authentication framework (BiometricPrompt). The application does not have its own PIN and only receives whether the authentication succeeded. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, transmit, or access any biometric data, including fingerprints or facial data, or the device's screen lock.

The Hide in recent apps option, in the same section, prevents the application's content, such as amounts and clients, from being visible in the recent apps screen.

Data Security

The information is kept in the application's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications, and benefits from the storage encryption of the device where it is available. To protect it further, it is recommended to use a screen lock on the device, to enable the app lock, and to keep backup files in a safe place.

Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it. The user can:

  • Edit or delete documents, payments, clients, saved items, and taxes from within the application. Deleted documents can be restored for a few seconds and are permanently removed after seven days
  • Remove the signature, and hide or replace the logo, from More → Template and brand
  • Delete backup files from the location where they were saved
  • Delete all the information by clearing the application's data from the device settings or by uninstalling the application

Backups, PDF documents, reports, and other files saved or shared by the user remain wherever the user saved or sent them until the user deletes them. Because Compose R.A Studio does not operate servers or user accounts, it does not hold a copy of the user's information that it could access or delete.

Information collected by Google's services is retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings. For requests about that information, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio, which will help direct them to the right place.

Because the information recorded in the application is stored only on the user's device, these rights can be exercised directly in the application: the information can be viewed and edited at any time, exported with a backup or a CSV report, and deleted as described above. The advertising choices can be exercised as described in Advertising and Privacy Choices. For any other request, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio using the details below.
